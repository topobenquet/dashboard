Formula parameters

How it is calculated

Weekly bonus = the greater of 3% and (ROAS − 1.1) × ((orders − 200) ÷ 100) × 10%, applied to that week's ad spend, and only if ROAS is above 1.1. It is then limited so that spend + bonus stays below 94% of sales, which sets a maximum of 0.94 × ROAS − 1.

Terms

  1. Agreed: ROAS is measured only with net Stripe sales divided by Google and Meta ad spend.
  2. Agreed: Monday to Sunday weeks, paid weekly, at least 50%.
  3. Agreed: the 6,000 and 10,000 reservations count from day one, Sep 24.
  4. Agreed: an order is only the US$ 29 reservation. Upsells do not count as orders, but their revenue adds to sales and to ROAS.
  5. Reference: at ROAS 1.3 the sales cap is 22%. A 40% bonus requires a ROAS of 1.49 or more.
