Privacy Policy

Finanzas JB · a personal financial reporting tool operated by Juan Benquet (JB Marketing / EME1 LLC) · Effective September 2, 2026 · Reviewed annually

1. What this application is

Finanzas JB is a private, single-user application. Its only user is its owner, Juan Benquet. It consolidates the owner's own bank, card and brokerage accounts into a monthly financial report. It is not offered to the public, has no customers, and collects no data from anyone other than the owner.

2. Data collected through Plaid

With the owner's explicit consent, given each time an account is connected through Plaid Link, the application retrieves from the connected financial institutions: account names and masked numbers, balances, transaction history (up to 24 months), and investment holdings. Plaid's own handling of this data is governed by the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

3. How the data is used

Exclusively to produce the owner's financial reports: categorizing income and expenses, tracking balances and cash flow, and summarizing investment positions. The application is read-only. It never initiates payments, transfers, or any change to any financial account.

4. Where the data is stored and who can access it

All data is stored on the owner's personal computer, protected by full-disk encryption (FileVault) and device authentication. Access tokens are kept in files readable only by the owner's operating-system account. Data is transmitted only between that computer and Plaid's API over TLS. No data is sold, shared, rented, or disclosed to any third party, and no third party has access to the device or the files.

5. Retention and deletion

Transaction data is retained on a rolling 24-month basis; older records are deleted. Access tokens are kept only while an account remains connected. The owner can disconnect any account at any time, which revokes the token at Plaid (/item/remove) and deletes the locally stored data for that account, and can also manage or revoke connections through the Plaid Portal. This policy is reviewed at least once a year.

6. Your rights and contact

Because the owner is the only data subject, all rights of access, correction and deletion are exercised directly by the owner. Questions about this policy: ppcmccjb@gmail.com.
